About Us

Located on the beautiful peninsula of Samaná, we offer professional, safe and unforgettable surf experiences. Whether it’s your first wave or you're looking to refine your skills, our experienced instructors are here to guide you in warm Caribbean waters.

FAQ

Do I need previous experience?

No experience needed. Our beginner lessons are perfect for first timers.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, towel, sunscreen and good energy. We provide boards and rash guards.

Are lessons safe?

Yes. Safety is our priority. All lessons begin with beach instruction and ocean awareness training.
